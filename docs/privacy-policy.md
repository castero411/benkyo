# Benkyo Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** November 19, 2026
**App:** Benkyo for Android (package `com.benkyo.app`)
**Developer:** {{DEVELOPER_NAME}}
**Contact:** ahking491@gmail.com

Benkyo is a study app with a pomodoro timer, tasks, reminders and calm backgrounds. This policy explains
what Benkyo does with your data. In short: **Benkyo does not collect, sell or share your data. Everything
you create stays on your device.**

## 1. Data we collect

We (the developer) **do not collect any data**. Benkyo has no account, no sign-in, no analytics, no
advertising, no crash reporting and no servers. The app does not send your data over the internet.

## 2. Data stored on your device

To work, Benkyo saves the following **only on your device**, in the app's private storage:

| Data | Why |
|---|---|
| Tasks (title, priority, due date and time, completion) | To show and sort your task list. |
| Focus session history (start, end, phase, linked task) | To keep a log of your study sessions. |
| Timer and app settings | To remember your preferences. |
| Scheduled reminders (title, time, repeat rule, linked task) | To notify you at the time you chose. |
| Backgrounds you add (copies of photos or videos you pick) | To show them behind the timer. |
| Spotify links you save | To open your playlists in Spotify. |

We cannot see or access this data.

## 3. Photos and videos

When you add a custom background, you choose files with the Android system photo picker. Benkyo can
only see the files you select, never the rest of your gallery. It does not ask for storage or photo
permissions. The selected files are copied into the app's private storage and are never uploaded.

## 4. Permissions

| Permission | Why |
|---|---|
| Notifications | To tell you when a timer phase ends, when a task is due and when a scheduled reminder fires, and to show the running timer. |
| Alarms and reminders (exact alarms) | To deliver those notifications on time. If you deny it, notifications may arrive a little late. |
| Run at startup | To restore your scheduled reminders after the phone restarts. |

Benkyo asks for each permission only when a feature needs it. You can change permissions at any time in
Android Settings.

## 5. Third parties

Benkyo does not share data with any third party. These apps and services may be involved when you use
certain features, under their own privacy policies:

- **Spotify:** when you tap a playlist, Benkyo opens it in the Spotify app. Only the playlist link is
  passed to Spotify. What happens next is covered by
  [Spotify's Privacy Policy](https://www.spotify.com/legal/privacy-policy/).
- **Google Play Store:** if Spotify isn't installed, Benkyo opens its Play Store page.
- **Android backup:** if you have turned on device backup, Android may include Benkyo's data in your
  encrypted Google backup so it can be restored on a new phone. This is controlled by you and Google,
  not by us. See [Google's Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy).

## 6. Data retention and deletion

Your data stays on your device until you delete it. You can delete individual tasks, reminders and
backgrounds in the app, or delete everything by clearing the app's storage or uninstalling Benkyo.
Since we hold no copy of your data, there is nothing for us to delete on our side.

## 7. Security

Data is kept in Benkyo's private app storage, which other apps cannot read. Because nothing is
transmitted, there is no data in transit to protect.

## 8. Children

Benkyo is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect data from anyone, including
children.

## 9. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the effective date above. Significant changes will
also be mentioned in the app's release notes.
